# Decision screen

The facility page at `/facilities/<id>` is where a decision maker makes the closure call. It shows the latest recommendation, the time left until its `decideBy` deadline, the reasoning behind it, and the facility's decision history. `Re-evaluate` pulls live conditions and loads a fresh recommendation. Accept, Override, and Defer each open a form with a `Recorded by` select that offers the facility's decision maker and then its backup, with a note that identity is unverified. Override and Defer require a reason. While the facility day has a current accept or override, a Directives section names that standing decision and can tell a building, the essential group, or one employee to do something other than the posture says. The API decides which decision is current, so a same-day re-evaluation leaves it standing until someone decides on the newer recommendation. Before anything is recorded, a confirmation step lists who will be notified, through which channel, by when, and who is left out and why. Once a decision sets a posture, a Notifications section shows its package and a `Dispatch notifications` action that records each notice and its lead time. When someone else decides or re-evaluates first, the submit is refused, the page reloads, and an alert says why.

## Sub-features

- `screen-recommendation` shows the posture as a labeled badge, `Confidence: <level>`, a `Live evaluation` or `As-of evaluation` label, the rationale, any thresholds crossed, caveats, inputs with their sources, and sensitivity statements. An early dismissal whose release time has passed says `release time has passed`.
- `screen-countdown` shows `Decide by <facility-local time>` and `<h> h <m> min left to decide`, or `Decision deadline passed <…> ago`, as text in a polite live region.
- `screen-reevaluate` posts a fresh recommendation, reloads the page with it, and says `New recommendation loaded.`. With no recommendation yet, the page says `No recommendation yet.` and offers only `Re-evaluate`. A new recommendation closes any open decision form, so a draft never posts against a recommendation it was not written for.
- `screen-accept`, `screen-override`, `screen-defer` record a decision. Override offers every posture but the recommended one. Defer offers re-check times of 15, 30, 60, or 120 min that fall before the deadline. With none left, `Defer` is disabled and the panel says `No re-check time fits before the decision deadline, so this cannot be deferred.`.
- `screen-reason-required` flags `Enter a reason.` on the `Reason` field, tied to it with `aria-describedby`, when Override or Defer is submitted without one, and records nothing. A change to a standing decision needs a reason even on Accept.
- `screen-recorded-by` offers exactly the facility's decision maker and backup, and shows `Identity unverified: …` under the select.
- `screen-recorded` shows `Decision recorded.` and the new row at the top of `Decision history`, with `(unverified)` after the recorder.
- `screen-failed` shows `The decision could not be recorded.` when the request never reaches the API or the answer is not the API's JSON, and puts the submit button back so the user can retry.
- `screen-directives` issues a directive for the whole facility, the essential group, a building, or one employee, with a required reason. The section says `Standing decision: <action>, <posture>, recorded <time>.` and, after an undecided same-day re-evaluation, `The recommendation above is newer and nobody has decided on it, so this decision still stands.`. Each listed directive shows its scope and kind, the release time of a timed dismissal, its reason category and text, and the issuer's recorded name and role. A building or employee name that two share carries `(ID <id>)`. A new current decision starts the directive draft over. Once the decision's notices are dispatched, the form gives way to a status saying a new directive would reach nobody. A stale tab whose directive the API refuses as `already_dispatched` shows `This decision's notices were dispatched before your directive arrived, …`, focused, and reloads.
- `screen-stale` shows `Someone else decided first.` when another decision landed after the page loaded, `A newer recommendation exists.` when a newer recommendation did, and `That recommendation was a preview, not the live one.` when the API refuses a decision on an as-of preview. Nothing is saved, the page reloads, and the alert appears only once the reload has finished.
- `screen-focus` keeps keyboard focus on purpose. An invalid submit focuses the top-most invalid control, which reads its error through `aria-describedby`. A recorded, refused, or failed submit focuses the notice that says so, and success text lands in a `role="status"` line that is always on the page. A finished Re-evaluate focuses the `Recommendation` heading, and a failed one returns focus to the button.
- `screen-earlier-day` treats a recommendation as decidable only when it is for the facility's current day, which the API names and which rolls over at closing time. A recommendation for an earlier day shows `This recommendation is for <date>. Re-evaluate for today.` with no `Decide` or `Directives` section. History stays. An open page reloads itself at `rollsOverAt`.
- `screen-backup` shows `Nobody decided before the deadline.` naming the backup decision maker when the newest history row is a missed deadline.
- `screen-confirm` opens `Confirm and notify` on every valid Record submit. It lists `Who will be notified` (name, channel, send-by time on the facility clock, and `Late` or `On time`) and `Not notified` (each person with a reason), and records nothing until `Record decision`. An override adds a note that the list is for the recommended posture; a deferral says it notifies nobody.
- `screen-confirm-late-recheck` sends a deferral back to its form with `Choose a re-check time before the decision deadline.` when the time spent in the dialog pushed its re-check to the deadline, and sends nothing. It needs a review of several minutes close to the deadline, so unit tests cover it and no step drives it.
- `screen-confirm-back` closes the dialog on `Back`, returns to the open form, and sends no decision.
- `screen-confirm-reload` closes an open `Confirm and notify` when a reload brings a different recommendation, so a confirmation is never recorded against one the manager did not review, and the decision form returns to work. Only a reload while the dialog is open shows it, such as the rollover at closing time, so unit tests cover it and no step drives it.
- `screen-notifications` shows a `Notifications` region for the facility's current decision as the server reports it (it stays current after a same-day re-evaluation and lapses when the facility day rolls over), with the decided package and `Dispatch notifications` when anyone needs a notice. After dispatch the page reloads, so `Record chain` checks the new rows, and it shows `Dispatched notices`, each with `Notified <n> min before the arrival window`. Before dispatching, the panel reads the package again; if who is told or how changed since it was shown, such as a late notice moving to the fastest channel, it shows the new list, says `Who is told, or how, changed since this list was shown. …`, and sends nothing until the next click. Only a send-by passing while the page is open stages that, so unit tests cover it and no step drives it. A dispatch that gets no clear answer says so and reloads to show whether it was recorded. Only a response lost after the write shows the reconciled page, which a blocked API cannot stage, so unit tests cover it and no step drives it. A deferral shows why there is nothing to notify.
- `screen-newer-recommendation` shows `A newer recommendation is available` with what changed in posture (including a delay length or release time), confidence, and hazard window, or that they match, when the newest decision was made on an older recommendation. It never changes the decision.
- `screen-chain` shows `Chain intact` under `Record chain` for the newest decision, or each break.
- `screen-api` serves `GET /api/facilities/:id/recorders` (the decision maker, then the backup, as `{id,name,role}`), `GET /api/facilities/:id/buildings` (each building with its employees' ids and names), and `GET /api/facilities/:id/facility-date` (`{facilityDate, rollsOverAt, currentDecisionId}`: the engine's facility day for now, the instant it next changes, and the facility's current decision by the directives rule, or `null`), each `404` for an unknown facility.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose a facility name in the `Facilities` table (see [facilities-list](./facilities-list.md)).
- Open `$WEB_URL/facilities/<id>` directly.
- Use a second browser tab on the same facility to act as another decision maker.

## Gotchas

- Every valid Record submit opens `Confirm and notify` first, outside the form, so recipes step back to the page with `within-page` before `click button "Record decision"`. An invalid submit shows its field error without the dialog.
- The confirmation list previews the recommended posture. An override's own package appears in `Notifications` after it is recorded, or, when the recommendation never timed that posture, the `untimed_posture` reason in its place.

- `expect-text` searches the whole page even inside `within`, and `block-api` affects only the tab it runs in.
- Recommendations are live, so the posture changes between runs. Pick `<other>` from the badge in `decision-screen-live`.
- The second tab is a separate page in the same browser run, so `tab` steps share one `browser.log`. Lines from the second tab carry its name, such as `[other]`.
- Every change after the first decision needs a reason, even an accept.
- Once an accept or override is current, the Directives form repeats `Reason category`, `Reason`, and `Recorded by`. Wrap decision-form steps in `within form "<form heading>"` and directive steps in `within form "Issue directive"`.
- The countdown updates on a 15-second tick and reads whole minutes, so two snapshots a few seconds apart can show the same text.
- Before the first evaluation, `GET …/recommendations/latest` answers `404`, so `browser.log` records a `console.error Failed to load resource … 404` line. A refused submit records a `409` line the same way, and a blocked one a `net::ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED` line. All are expected.
